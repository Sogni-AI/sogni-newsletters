Behind the scenes

Eight models. One connected experience.

Claude Code and Codex agents built the experience with a human directing the work and reviewing what shipped. The art, films, music, voices and collectible 3D assets were generated on the Sogni Supernet. Here is how the pieces fit.

01   Make a place—and keep its character

Krea 2 Turbo draws the scenes. Krea 2 Identity Edit uses reference images to keep Simon recognizable as the setting changes.

02   Give a choice somewhere to go

SAM 3 selects the objects you can click. MiniMax H3 / FastH3 turns the departure and destination pictures into a journey with sound.

03   Make the world sound like itself

MiniMax Music 3 supplies the score. Qwen3-TTS gives recurring characters a consistent voice.

04   Turn a discovery over in your hands

BiRefNet separates an object from its background. Pixal3D reconstructs it as a textured 3D collectible.

Your turn

Start with one scene and two choices.

You do not need to plan an entire game. Imagine a place, give visitors two interesting things to click, and decide what each choice reveals. A tiny world with a good surprise is a great first project.
