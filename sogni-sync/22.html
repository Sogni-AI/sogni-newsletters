The headline · 2K Beta · Included in Unlimited

The second stage changes the picture.

MiniMax H3’s open-weight release arrived without the second stage behind MiniMax’s hosted high-resolution output. The community built its own approach. We’ve built on that work and optimized it for the Sogni Supernet, bringing H3 to stunning 2K with dialogue and sound intact.

The second pass enlarges and refines the video before it becomes the finished picture. Look at the wood grain, lettering and fabric in the 2K coffee-bar demos. Credit to LBH-123-AI’s community latent upscaler, the foundation of Sogni’s optimized implementation.

H3 also gives you four speed choices: Standard, Balanced, LightX2V Turbo and FastH3 Turbo. FastH3 is our fastest tier for everyday iteration; choose two-stage 2K when you want the extra detail. The four-tier speed test and upscaling comparison let you judge the results yourself.

Creative control

Bring your own style. Keep your character.

Bring Your Own LoRA is here. Unlimited subscribers can import compatible LoRAs from Civitai or Hugging Face into My LoRAs and use them alongside the catalog. Bring a look you love into Sogni without managing a local generation setup.

Krea 2 Identity Edit helps carry a recognizable person or character into a new outfit, pose or scene. Give it reference images and describe the change. It is a useful partner for a consistent campaign—or a recurring pink sloth.

Included in Unlimited

Sound, motion, objects—and what comes next.

Every model below is included in Sogni Unlimited under fair use. Came for one model? Try another this week.

Give it a voice

MiniMax Music 3 brings songs and instrumental scores. Qwen3-TTS adds studio voices, cloning from a recording, and voice design from a description. Then hand the audio to H3’s new Sound to Video: FastH3 animates to your clip, lips included.

Keep the scene going

Every finished video in Sogni Web now has Continue this video, which starts your next clip from its exact last frame. Join the shots in the Clip Mixer with transitions and music.

Direct another kind of motion

LTX 2.5 brings multi-shot prompting: connected scenes and hard cuts from one prompt.

Take an image into 3D

Pixal3D turns one image—or multiple views of the same subject—into a textured 3D model for games, scenes and AR.

Select, separate, finish

SAM 3 selects a particular object. BiRefNet removes the background with soft edges for hair and fur. RTX VSR adds prompt-free image upscaling, and FlashVSR upscales finished videos to 1080p or 1440p.

A smarter prompt assistant

Unlimited members now get DeepSeek V4 Flash as their Prompt Assistant and AI Screenwriter, so expanded prompts and video scripts follow your idea more faithfully.
