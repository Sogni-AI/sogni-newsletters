## Sustainable cadence

Use a **weekly Thursday /Sync**, normally 400–700 words, with one lead story, up to three supporting modules, and one community item when available. The September catch-up is intentionally longer. Let a focused walkthrough replace a roundup when it is more interesting. Date the edition by publication, and use “since our last issue” when catching up.

Keep one small release inbox with: live date, reader benefit, evidence link, existing visual, intended audience, billing/access distinction, and next-edition placement. Add to it when work ships; spend 15 minutes weekly selecting rather than reconstructing two months of history. One editor owns the draft, Mark gives final copy approval, and copy freezes 48 hours before the intended send. New launches after freeze normally move forward; corrections to prices, eligibility, offers or availability still get fixed before dispatch. If time is tight, shorten the issue and keep the date.

Judge the restart by delivered volume, complaints/unsubscribes, bot-filtered clicks to the lead story, return-to-app activity and trial/paid conversion where already measurable. Opens are a secondary signal. Current subscriber milestone, paid-versus-trial definition and send audience/engagement remain to be confirmed; none is invented here. Mark's “6,0000” needs clarification between 6,000 and 60,000 before using it in public copy. The release plan records his final media selections.
